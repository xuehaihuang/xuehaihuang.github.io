<header class="page-header">
  <div class="container narrow">
    <p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
    <h1>Discretizing structure, not only equations.</h1>
    <p class="page-lead">My research develops finite element spaces and numerical methods that retain the differential, geometric, and mechanical structure of partial differential equations.</p>
  </div>
</header>

<section class="section">
  <div class="container research-list">
    <article class="research-topic">
      <div class="topic-index">01</div>
      <div>
        <h2>Finite Element Complexes</h2>
        <p>Differential complexes organize operators, kernels, ranges, and stability conditions into a coherent framework. My work studies conforming, nonconforming, and distributional discretizations of de Rham, Stokes, elasticity, Hessian, div-div, curl-div, and conformal complexes.</p>
        <ul class="tag-list"><li>finite element exterior calculus</li><li>BGG constructions</li><li>commuting diagrams</li><li>discrete exactness</li></ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="research-topic">
      <div class="topic-index">02</div>
      <div>
        <h2>Tensor Finite Elements</h2>
        <p>Tensor-valued problems require finite elements with carefully designed traces and continuity. I construct symmetric, traceless, and constrained tensor elements using geometric decompositions, tangential-normal bases, normal-normal continuity, and hybridizable formulations.</p>
        <ul class="tag-list"><li>symmetric tensors</li><li>traceless tensors</li><li>tangential-normal decomposition</li><li>higher-order variables</li></ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="research-topic">
      <div class="topic-index">03</div>
      <div>
        <h2>Mixed, Nonconforming & Hybrid Methods</h2>
        <p>I study stable low-order methods that reduce regularity requirements while preserving the essential analytical structure of the model. These include mixed and nonconforming finite elements, virtual element methods, hybridization, and staggered discontinuous Galerkin methods.</p>
        <ul class="tag-list"><li>mixed methods</li><li>nonconforming FEM</li><li>virtual elements</li><li>staggered DG</li><li>hybridization</li></ul>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="research-topic">
      <div class="topic-index">04</div>
      <div>
        <h2>Numerical Methods for PDEs</h2>
        <p>The constructions are motivated by concrete models in solid and fluid mechanics and by higher-order equations. Applications include linear and strain-gradient elasticity, Stokes and Brinkman systems, quad-curl problems, plate models, polyharmonic equations, and singular perturbations.</p>
        <ul class="tag-list"><li>elasticity</li><li>Stokes systems</li><li>fourth-order PDEs</li><li>singular perturbations</li><li>polyharmonic problems</li></ul>
      </div>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="statement-band">
  <div class="container narrow">
    <p>Across these themes, the aim is the same: identify the right analytical structure first, then turn it into finite element spaces and algorithms that are stable, accurate, and implementable.</p>
  </div>
</section>
